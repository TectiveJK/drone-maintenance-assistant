Register drones and equipment notes.

Mark each item PASS / FAIL / N/A, then save.

Maintenance Tasks

Faults / Incidents

Reports

Fleet and maintenance summary stored on this device.


        


        

          

            
Install on iPhone or iPad

            
This is a web companion you can pin to your Home Screen. Data stays in Safari on this device (separate from the Linux desktop database).

          

          

            
1. Open in Safari

            
Use Safari on your iPhone or iPad (Chrome/Firefox on iOS cannot add Home Screen apps the same way).

          

          

            
2. Share → Add to Home Screen

            
Tap the Share button, scroll to Add to Home Screen, then tap Add. Launch from the new icon for a full-screen app experience.

          

          

            
3. Serve from your computer

            
On the machine that has this project, run ./scripts/serve-ios.sh, then open the printed URL on your device (same Wi‑Fi).
